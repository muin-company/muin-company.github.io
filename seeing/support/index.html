Seeing Tonight — Support

Updated 27 October 2026

Write to seeing@muin.company. Tell me which place and which night, and I can usually see what you saw.

What the app answers

A weather app answers whether it will rain. An observer asks something else: where is the sky open tonight, and when does it actually get dark. Seeing Tonight shows low, mid and high cloud hour by hour for the places you observe from, the moment astronomical twilight ends, the seeing in arcseconds, and how high the moon and planets climb.

Why three cloud layers instead of one number

One total is not enough. A single solid deck hides the sky whether or not anything sits above it, while 70% high cloud can still be a workable night. The three bands are drawn separately so you can tell those apart.

Why twilight and not sunset

The number you plan around is astronomical twilight, when the sun is 18° below the horizon. In October in Seoul the sky stays blue for about an hour and a quarter after sunset, and faint objects are not visible in it. The app computes that moment from the site's coordinates.

Where the data comes from

  • MET Norway — cloud in three layers, temperature, humidity, dew point, wind. Published under CC BY 4.0.
  • 7Timer! — seeing and transparency.
  • Sunset, twilight, moonrise, moon phase and planet altitudes are computed on the device, not fetched.

The app shows two models side by side on the same axis, and counts the hours where they differ by more than 20 points. One model shown alone looks like fact; models disagree often.

The night theme

Black ground and red text, for use at a dark site. It is not only recoloured: under red light hue cannot carry meaning, so the cloud bands become a red brightness scale and the planet lines are told apart by dash pattern.
